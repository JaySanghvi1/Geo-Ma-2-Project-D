How to Build a Smart Fest Budget (Class IX)

  1. Set your goal: safe, fun, and affordable fest.
  2. List expected income sources and realistic rates.
  3. Separate fixed and variable expenses before deciding final plans.
  4. Add a small buffer for surprises (rain, low crowd, repair needs).
  5. Check opportunity cost: choosing one extra cost means giving up another use of money.
  6. Use cost-control ideas: compare suppliers, borrow reusable items, and avoid waste.

Assumptions used here: this is a one-day school fest simulation, prices are sample estimates, and all figures are for learning.
